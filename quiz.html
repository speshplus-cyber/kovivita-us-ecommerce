Frequently Asked Sizing Questions

How do I measure ceiling clearance with a tree topper? keyboard_arrow_down

We recommend leaving at least 6 to 12 inches between the top tip of your tree and the ceiling. For example, if your room has an 8-foot ceiling (96 inches), a 7ft or 7.5ft tree leaves 6 to 12 inches for your traditional angel or star topper without cramping your room crown molding.

Why does American True-Needle PE last for over 20+ holiday seasons? keyboard_arrow_down

Polyethylene (PE) molded tips maintain their architectural memory season after season. They do not crush, warp in storage, or discolor under room ambient lighting. Our dual-steel core branches support even the heaviest heirloom glass ornaments without sagging.

How does US White-Glove delivery and fulfillment work? keyboard_arrow_down

All Kovivita trees ship directly from our climate-controlled American distribution centers in New Jersey and Southern California. Orders over $50 include complimentary insured ground freight arriving in heavy-duty reinforced storage cartons with protective luggage handles.
