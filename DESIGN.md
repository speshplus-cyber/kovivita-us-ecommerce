## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium, heritage-infused e-commerce atmosphere tailored for the discerning American homeowner. The aesthetic bridges Britain’s premier botanical craftsmanship with high-end American lifestyle commerce—evoking warmth, generational longevity, and refined holiday magic without ever veering into kitsch.

The aesthetic philosophy merges **Modern Minimalist Luxury** with **Subtle Glassmorphism** and **Tactile Depth**:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, festive, architectural, and warm.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep alpine forest textures, lustrous gilded accents, and crisp, generous negative space.
- **Sensory cues:** Heavyweight editorial layout rhythm, whisper-soft background blurs on floating bars, fine jewelry-level line weights, and polished micro-interactions.

## Colors

The palette relies on a balance between regal British holiday tradition and high-ticket American retail expectations:

- **Primary (`#9E182F` Crimson Velvet):** Used for primary conversion hooks, badges, high-impact seasonal banners, and key calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#1B4332` Deep Woodland Pine):** Evokes dense Fraser fir and realistic PE/PVC foliage. Used for authority elements, guarantee seals, and grounding footers.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` Warm Gilded Glow):** Deployed selectively for star ratings, VIP tiers, foil-stamped badges, and focus accents.
- **Neutrals (`#18181B` Charcoal Pine & `#FAFAF9` Warm Snow):** High-contrast editorial typography sits on luminous, warm-white canvases instead of sterile clinical white, creating an inviting domestic gallery feel.

### Role Assignments
- **Surfaces:** Default backdrop is `canvas-cream` (`#FAFAF9`), transitioning to pure `#FFFFFF` for product card containers to establish clean product presentation.
- **Borders:** Low-opacity `border-hairline` (`#E4E4E7`) ensures structural integrity without visual noise.

## Typography

The type system pairs high-end editorial gravitas with friction-free modern transactional utility.

- **Headlines & Display (Playfair Display):** Conveys bespoke craftsmanship, arboreal heritage, and timeless luxury. Numbers in Playfair Display are avoided for commerce specs to maintain readability; italic variations are reserved for emotive secondary clauses (e.g., *“Crafted for Generations”*).
- **Body, Specs, & UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides legible, geometrically clean metrics, foliage branch counts, tip-density numbers, height tags (e.g., `7.5 ft`), and USD pricing tiers.
- **Letter Spacing:** Generous kerning (`0.12em`) on uppercase kicker tags (e.g., `10-YEAR FOLIAGE GUARANTEE`) injects haute-couture packaging aesthetics into utility headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to a flexible 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around luxury whitespace and content density:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** Max-width container capped at `1440px` with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Product comparison tools span full width with pinned visual columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins. Filter sidebars collapse into a slide-over canvas.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins. Tree height selector chips and gallery thumbnails convert to snap-to-edge horizontal carousels.

Vertical rhythm follows an 8pt increment model. High-impact seasonal showcases leverage `space-2xl` breathing room to frame tree silhouettes without visual claustrophobia.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through frosted glass and soft, low-intensity shadows rather than stark structural drop shadows:

- **Level 1 (Product Cards & Specs):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `canvas-cream` background with a subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04)` combined with a 1px border (`#E4E4E7`).
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Height Select Popovers, Tooltips):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08)`, 1px border (`rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08)`).
- **Glassmorphic Sticky Utilities (Global Navigation & US Guarantee Bar):** Background uses `rgba(250, 250, 249, 0.88)` with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur and bottom border `1px solid rgba(228, 228, 231, 0.6)`. Gives navigation an unobtrusive presence during deep product browsing.
- **Hero & Floating Badges:** Subtly recessed inner border highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) to convey fine embossed stationery.

## Shapes

The design uses a tailored, architectural roundedness level:
- Base elements (cards, text fields, notification panels) use **`0.25rem` (4px)** radii, communicating precision engineering and architectural permanence.
- Contextual interactive controls (height filter pills, guarantee badges) use specialized pill styling to contrast against square product cards.
- Imagery utilizes strict `4px` corner rounding with clean image aspect ratios (4:5 for trees, 1:1 for detail foliage macros).

## Components

### Buttons & Primary Triggers
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Cart", "Design Your Tree"):** Deep Crimson (`#9E182F`) background, `#FFFFFF` Plus Jakarta Sans text (Semi-bold, 14px), 4px border radius. Hover state shifts to `#801224` with a subtle transform lift (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary Action ("Compare Foliage", "Quick View"):** Transparent background, `1px solid #18181B`, dark charcoal text. Hover fills with `#18181B` and inverts text to `#FAFAF9`.
- **Text Link with Ornament:** Pine green (`#1B4332`) with a gold underline offset that expands on hover.

### Height Selection & Filter Pills
- Horizontal array of micro-buttons representing tree stature: `4.5ft`, `6ft`, `7.5ft`, `9ft`, `10ft+`.
- **Default:** Transparent background, `1px solid #E4E4E7`, charcoal text.
- **Selected:** Deep Forest Pine (`#1B4332`) fill, crisp white text, accompanied by an absolute badge displaying tip density or LED count.

### Luxury Retail Product Cards
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` fill, 4px radius, `1px solid #E4E4E7` boundary.
- **Visuals:** 4:5 aspect ratio photography on neutral studio glow. Secondary lifestyle hover image reveals lit ambient night mode.
- **Metadata Stack:**
  - Micro-kicker: 10-Year Foliage Guarantee icon + text in `label-caps`.
  - Title in `Playfair Display` (`headline-sm`).
  - Star ratings: 5-star cluster in `#D4AF37` followed by Trustpilot count (`4.9/5 · 2,400+ Reviews`).
  - Price: Prominent USD format (`$549.00`) alongside financing notice (`Or $49/mo via Affirm`).

### Value Proposition & Guarantee Banner
- Sticky top announcement strip styled in Forest Pine (`#1B4332`):
  - Segment 1: "Free Contiguous US Ground Shipping on Orders $50+".
  - Segment 2: "10-Year Master Foliage Guarantee".
  - Segment 3: "Real-Needle PE Ultra-Realistic Feel".
  - Separated by hairline gold diamonds (`♦`).

### Interactive Tree Comparison Matrix
- Comparative columns with sticky tree thumbnail headers.
- Visual rows evaluating: Branch Tip Ratio (% PE vs. PVC), Lighting Tech (Warm Glow LED vs Dual-Color), Trunk Assembly (PowerConnect Pole), and Girth Dimensions.
